About the sources of this book

The mathematics and the history are standard and can be checked in the classical texts: Haykin, Communication Systems; Lathi & Ding, Modern Digital and Analog Communication Systems; Proakis & Salehi, Fundamentals of Communication Systems; Cover & Thomas, Elements of Information Theory; Papoulis & Pillai, Probability, Random Variables and Stochastic Processes; Lin & Costello, Error Control Coding; and Shannon's own 1948 paper, which is remarkably readable. The exam questions come from the official GATE EC question papers (2014, 2019–2026) listed in Chapter 13. The figures in the book are computed live in your browser by the same formulas you study (Bessel functions, error functions, FFTs and Monte-Carlo simulations); nothing is a stock image.
