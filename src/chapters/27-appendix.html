The mathematics and the history are standard and can be checked in the classical texts. Core textbooks: S. Haykin, Communication Systems; B. P. Lathi & Z. Ding, Modern Digital and Analog Communication Systems; J. G. Proakis & M. Salehi, Digital Communications and Fundamentals of Communication Systems; B. Sklar, Digital Communications: Fundamentals and Applications; A. Papoulis & S. U. Pillai, Probability, Random Variables and Stochastic Processes; A. V. Oppenheim & A. S. Willsky, Signals and Systems. Information and coding: C. E. Shannon, “A mathematical theory of communication” (1948, remarkably readable); T. M. Cover & J. A. Thomas, Elements of Information Theory; R. G. Gallager, Information Theory and Reliable Communication; D. J. C. MacKay, Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms; S. Lin & D. J. Costello, Error Control Coding; T. Richardson & R. Urbanke, Modern Coding Theory; A. J. Viterbi & J. K. Omura, Principles of Digital Communication and Coding; E. Arıkan, “Channel polarization” (2009). Wireless and systems: D. Tse & P. Viswanath, Fundamentals of Wireless Communication; A. Goldsmith, Wireless Communications; T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice; J. G. Andrews et al. on 5G; J. J. Spilker, Digital Communications by Satellite; E. D. Kaplan & C. Hegarty, Understanding GPS. The exam questions come from the official GATE EC question papers (2014, 2019–2026) listed in Chapter 24 and from solved-paper collections for 2015–2018.
