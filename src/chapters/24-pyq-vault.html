Patterns in the wording of questions
  • "rounded off to two decimal places" NATs have a tolerance in the key (typically ±1–2%); do not over-compute, but do not round early.
  • Several questions say "use the given approximation" (2014 Set 4 Q.52 with $Q(v)\approx e^{-v^2/2}$; 2026 Q.44 with $\erfc(u)\approx e^{-u^2}/(u\sqrt\pi)$). Use that formula, even when it differs from the exact value.
  • Units: 2021 Q.52 wanted the SQNR as a ratio; 2024 Q.57 wanted dB; 2026 Q.55 compared capacity to $B$, not in bit/s. Read the requested unit before computing.
  • MSQ questions give marks only for the exact right set (no negative marking, no partial marks in the keys I saw): don't tick doubtful options.
